Unicode Letter Tracing

Traces every frame of a video with the outline of the closest Unicode character, chosen from 410,510 glyphs. You decide how many letters fill the screen.

Your video is never uploaded. Analysis and export run entirely in this browser.

Download the glyph data

To redraw videos in your browser, this site needs its glyph dictionary: the shapes and matching data of all 410,510 glyphs. It is downloaded once and kept in this browser, so later visits load it without downloading again.

How characters are chosen: each cell is reduced to 32×24 and blurred, then compared with 410,510 glyphs rendered from all 244 Noto, GNU Unifont and Jigmo fonts (Unicode 17, 155,230 code points). The search is exhaustive over features compressed to 128 dimensions with PCA.
